Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

In short: Perly has no accounts and no servers of its own. Everything you log about your cycle and health is stored only on your phone. We never see it, sell it or share it with advertisers.

This policy explains how the Perly app ("Perly", "we", "us"), published by Fidovasoft, handles information. Contact: fidovasoft@gmail.com.

1. Health and cycle data you enter

Period dates and flow, symptoms, mood, notes, basal body temperature, weight, water, sleep, intimacy, pill and test results, medications, tags, pregnancy mode dates, doctor appointments, reminders and app settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party. You can also protect the app with a PIN.

2. Backups and exports

When you choose to export (JSON backup, CSV, calendar file or text summary), Perly creates the file on your device and opens the system share menu. Where the file goes, for example Google Drive, email or another app, is your choice and is governed by that service's privacy policy. Importing a file reads it locally only.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device and usage information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device model, app interactions and crash diagnostics. Perly never passes your health or cycle data to AdMob.

  • Ads are non-personalized by default. You can allow personalized ads in Settings.
  • Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), Google's consent form is shown before ads are requested.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Purchases (Premium)

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iOS. Perly talks to them directly on your device to check whether Premium is active; there is no other payment or billing service and we never receive your payment details. See the Google privacy policy and the Apple privacy policy.

5. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. With discreet notifications enabled, notification text contains no health details.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Use Profile, then Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data. Purchase and advertising data held by Google or Apple is handled under their retention policies and can be requested from them.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. Your in-app data is fully under your control on your device. For questions about ad or purchase data, contact us and we will help you reach the right provider.

9. Children

Perly is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Security

Data is stored in the app's sandboxed storage and protected by your device encryption. An optional PIN adds a lock to the app. Keep your exported backup files in a secure place.

11. Medical disclaimer

Perly is not a medical device and must not be used as contraception. Predictions are estimates. Always consult a healthcare professional.

12. Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
